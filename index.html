About Benvor Digital

Growth marketing, run like a business — because we've run one

We started Benvor Digital because too many agencies sell activity instead of outcomes. We built ours around the opposite idea.

How we started

Benvor Digital was founded by a small team of in‑house marketers who kept getting pulled into agency reviews for the wrong reasons — vague reporting, generic strategy decks, and campaigns that looked busy but didn't move revenue. We built the agency we wished we'd had: one that treats every client's budget like it's coming out of our own pocket.

Today we work with local shops opening their first location and global brands running campaigns across a dozen markets. The size changes. The standard doesn't.

Our mission

To make sophisticated, ROI‑driven marketing accessible to businesses of every size — not just the ones with enterprise budgets and in‑house data teams.

What makes us different

  • One team handles strategy, design, and media — nothing gets lost in a handoff between departments.
  • You get access to the same live dashboard we use internally, not a curated monthly summary.
  • Every contract is quarter‑to‑quarter. We keep clients with results, not paperwork.
  • We say no to channels that won't work for your business, even if it means a smaller invoice.
